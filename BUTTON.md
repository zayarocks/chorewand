# Home-Made "Easy" Button

## Materials
- [ ] cardboard
- [ ] conductive tape (copper foil)
- [ ] non-conductive tape (painter's)

## Tools
- [ ] scissors
- [ ] multimeter

## Assembly

1. Cut a rectangular strip of cardboard.
2. Loosely fold the cardboard in half, forming a V shape.
3. Apply copper foil tape to both inside surfaces, aligning the contacts.
4. Leave a small exposed copper tab extending from each contact for testing.
5. Secure any loose edges with painter's tape, keeping the contacts separated when released.

## Testing

Verify continuity across the copper contacts. The circuit should be closed when the button is pressed and open when released.

**HOORAY!** You made a button!
